# Barrel Proof — audit and share-readiness pass

_September 2026. Branch `claude/whisky-app-audit-39vgtv`._

The goal was to get Barrel Proof to the point where a group of friends can open
it on their phones and trust what it tells them. This document records what
was examined, what was found, what changed, and what is still worth doing.

## Launch scope

After reviewing the verdict matrix below, the Buy / Consider / Pass engine
ships **switched off**. Its anchors are control-state list prices, which run
below open-market shelves such as New York's, so an ordinary local price can
read as "Pass" and cost the app a friend's trust on first use. The launch is a
shelf, tasting and games companion: Bottles (lookup, scan, bottle cards with
list prices and price sightings), Shelf, Tastings, Night, Showdown, For You,
Cocktails, Distilleries and Club. The engine and its tests remain in the repo
behind More → Price check · beta, and every price sighting the group logs
builds the local price map that will make it trustworthy.

## Summary

Barrel Proof's logic layer was already in good shape: 251 focused tests, clean
separation between rendering and decision logic, a careful catalog pipeline
with tie-out gates, and thoughtful touches everywhere (focus preservation while
typing, self-healing service-worker caches, honest handling of degraded boots).

What kept it from being shareable was a handful of things a builder never sees
on their own device:

1. **Every friend became "Joe".** The palate, the name on blind flights, and
   the club card were hard-coded to one person.
2. **First paint waited on Google Fonts.** With the CDN unreachable, the app
   hung on a blank screen for 14 seconds before its own code even ran.
3. **Store Mode could not find most Weller expressions.** Search reused the
   collection wizard's batch-line grouping, which folds every Weller into one
   "Weller 12Y" row; "Rare Breed" ranked two ryes above the bourbon; a $15,000
   typo sat in the top five for "eagle rare".
4. **The verdict engine had two silent biases.** Unknown palate, club, and
   review signals were filled with neutral 0.5s, so a bottle well under its
   typical price could never be a Buy for someone who hadn't logged pours yet.
   And "palate fit" was shown as a low percentage on the 99% of catalog rows
   that carry no flavor tags, with a caution attached, on every scorecard.
5. **Ten tabs, a developer QA tool among them, in a four-row grid** taking a
   third of a phone screen, with "Reset everything" one tap from the header.
6. **Trading club cards meant emailing JSON files** between phones.

All six are fixed on this branch, along with a longer list below. The test
suite is at 272 passing tests (from 251), and every change was verified in a
headless Chromium walkthrough at phone and desktop sizes.

## How the audit was done

- Read every module in `src/`, `tools/build-imported-catalog.js`, the service
  worker, the stylesheet, and the tests.
- Rendered the app with Playwright at 390×844 (iPhone, touch) and 1440×900,
  walking all ten tabs, the scorecard with and without a price, search, and
  the first-run flows; captured console output, DOM size, page heights, and
  timings.
- CPU-profiled boot with the Chrome DevTools protocol to attribute the
  14-second first paint.
- Ran the real 8,541-bottle index through Store Mode search for 20 queries a
  friend would type ("weller", "eagle rare", "blanton", "ecbp", "stagg", …) and
  inspected the top five and the collapse groups behind them.
- Ran the decision engine over realistic shelf prices for 21 bottles (three
  price points each) with a neutral palate and no club, and read every verdict.
- Measured the first-load payload per asset (raw and gzip) and the byte cost of
  each field in the catalog index.

## Findings and changes

### Share blockers

| Finding | Evidence | Change |
|---|---|---|
| One hard-coded person. `palate.name = "Joe"` seeded flights, club cards, and every palate-fit score. | `src/data/bottles.js`; `renderNight`, `saveFlight`, `exportClubCard` | New per-device **profile** (`src/logic/profile.js`, state schema v11): first-run sheet asks name, proof comfort, and up to six flavors; the palate is derived from it; editable from the header chip or More. Seed palate is now neutral. |
| Render-blocking third-party fonts. | CPU profile: 12.7 s idle waiting on `fonts.googleapis.com`; JS boot itself ~0.7 s | Fraunces (opsz+wght) and Inter (wght) **self-hosted** as variable woff2 (115 KB total, OFL), preloaded, `font-display: swap`, precached by the service worker. Boot on a blocked network: 14 s → under 1 s. |
| Weller family collapsed to one search row. | "weller" → 3 rows for 71 listings; "line:weller" group of 86 | `BarrelCollection.productKey()`: product-level identity for search (sizes, years, batch codes, pick markers, shorthand stripped; ages/proofs/"single barrel" kept; brand rules for Blanton's, Eagle Rare, Weller). The wizard keeps `lineKey()`. "weller" now returns Antique 107, 12 Year, Special Reserve, Full Proof, C.Y.P.B., Single Barrel as separate rows. |
| Club cards exchanged as files. | `exportClubCard` / `importClubCard` only | **Share links**: the card is deflate-compressed and base64url-encoded into `#club=…` (a 60-rating card is ~1.4 KB). Native share sheet on phones, clipboard fallback, paste-a-link field, and an "Add to my club" confirmation when a link opens the app. File export/import kept as the fallback. |
| Developer QA tab and destructive reset exposed. | "Data" tab: 27,000 px tall, 1,282 nodes; reset icon in header | Data tools hidden behind a toggle in More (`?dev=1` also works). Reset moved to the bottom of More with its confirmation. |

### Correctness of the decision engine

| Finding | Evidence | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Price anchor was whichever state's price the importer met first. | 47% of multi-price bottles had a first price >15% from the range midpoint; "Eagle Rare Straight Bourbon" first price $15,000 next to a $150 minimum | `getSourceRetailPriceInfo()`: tight range → midpoint ("Typical state retail (N prices)", medium confidence at 3+); wide range → the low cluster; single → as-is. |
| Unknown signals filled with 0.5 capped verdicts. | With neutral palate and no club, max score at a "Strong" price was 0.63 → Consider | Verdict is a weighted average over **known** signals only; `result.evidence` records which ones, and the scorecard says "Judged on price alone so far" when that is the case. |
| Fake palate fit on untagged bottles. | 8,533 of 8,541 rows have no `profile` tags; fit rendered as e.g. 16–27% with a caution | `getPalateFit()`: tags+favorites → real fit; learned profile (once anything is logged, or from the first-run profile) → learned fit; proof-only → shown as "Proof fit"; otherwise unknown, neutral, no caution. |
| Allocated bottles judged like shelf bourbon. | Weller 12 at $65 → Pass (hype penalty against the MSRP guardrail); Elmer T. Lee at $45 → Pass | No hype penalty against the MSRP guardrail; allocation bands (1.25× / 2× / 3× retail) for allocated rows that only carry a state list price; the availability model's brand knowledge (Stagg, Elmer T. Lee, Blanton's…) is passed into the verdict; near-retail on an allocated bottle is a Buy with the economics spelled out. |
| Standard bottles flagged allocated by their aliases. | 53 rows (Knob Creek, 1792 Small Batch, Elijah Craig Small Batch…) were "allocated" only because name and aliases were concatenated before pattern matching, producing phantom hits like "batch 1792", or because a merge dragged a "15YR" alias under the 9-year bottle | Patterns run against the display name; aliases may only vouch for brand-level tokens without digits. |
| Paying secondary price read as a Buy. | Blanton's at $150 vs $160 secondary → Buy | Secondary bands are now 0.7× / 1.0× / 1.2×, and matching the market is "the going rate, not a deal". |
| For You ignored the stated taste. | After onboarding: "Your palate profile is still empty"; every rationale identical | Profile seeds the recommender (flavors, proof) until pours take over; rationale now reads "hits your cherry & oak notes; right in your proof lane". |

Verdict matrix after the changes (neutral palate, no club): Weller 12 $65 Buy / $120 Pass; Weller Antique $55 Buy / $90 Consider / $165 Pass; Blanton's $70 Buy / $110 Consider; Stagg $70 Buy / $300 Pass; George T. Stagg $150 Buy / $400 Pass; Buffalo Trace $28 Consider / $42 Pass; Rare Breed $50 Buy / $68 Consider / $95 Pass; Old Forester 1920 $60 Buy / $110 Pass; Four Roses Small Batch $32 Consider / $50 Pass.

### Search

| Finding | Change |
|---|---|
| Ranking favored long, evidence-heavy spellings; "rare breed" put two ryes first. | Ranking now weighs how much of the name the query covers, penalizes extra tokens, boosts groups that contain a curated record, and sinks $1,500+ reps for generic queries. Flagships rank first for all 20 test queries. |
| Multi-word queries had to be contiguous ("pappy 15" found nothing). | Every word must appear, in any order, on every search surface (Store, Quick add, Tastings picker, Night, Showdown, identity link). |
| Store picks leaked through ("Buy-The-Barrel", "Priv Selection", "Barrel Program", "Brrl Select", "FWGS Exclusive"). | Pick detection extended; still one tap to reveal. |
| A 375 ml or 1.75 L listing could front a product group and set the wrong price basis. | Standard size is now decisive in representative selection; non-standard sizes are labeled on result rows. |
| Distributors shown as makers ("Bacardi U.S.A., Inc.", "Intercontinental Packaging Company"). | Supplier-like strings fall through to the distillery taxonomy (Angel's Envy, 2XO, Bulleit…). |
| Numeric query words matched inside other numbers ("15" hit "115 proof" and record ids). | Numeric tokens match whole numbers only. |
| Seed bottles that absorbed catalog rows at boot kept none of the rows' evidence, so "Eagle Rare 17" outranked "Eagle Rare 10". | Boot merge now sums source counts, unions regions and source ids, and inherits the evidence grade; generic brand queries also nudge unicorns one row down. |

## Deliberately unchanged

- The catalog data itself. Anomalies found along the way (a $15,000 Michigan
  price, "Eagle Rare SP Bourbon", "Weller Millenium Blend99p") are handled by
  ranking and anchoring rather than edited by hand; the raw imports are not in
  this repository, so the pipeline could not be re-run.
- The seeded price bands and curated overlays. They are opinions the owner
  chose; the engine now uses them more consistently.
- Visual language. The brass-on-mahogany system, typography, motion, and
  card grammar are strong. New surfaces (sheets, bottom bar, empty states,
  onboarding) were built in that language rather than replacing it.

## Recommended next steps

1. **Deploy**: Settings → Pages → deploy from `main` (root). The app is ready
   as a static site; nothing else is required.
2. **Curate the top 40 hunt bottles.** Secondary-market data exists for only a
   handful of records. Attaching DramValue averages to Blanton's, Weller 12,
   Eagle Rare, Stagg, E.H. Taylor, and the BTAC line would turn "allocated
   economics" heuristics into real numbers. Data tools → DramValue queue lists
   them.
3. **Re-run the catalog pipeline** with the slimming step on, and consider
   emitting a median price per bottle (the index only carries min/max/count).
4. **Product photos.** Only LCBO-matched bottles have real photos. A curated
   set for the 100 most-viewed bottles would lift the whole app.
5. **Observed prices from the club.** Cards could carry each friend's price
   sightings so the group builds a local price map together.
6. **iOS install prompt.** A one-time "Add to Home Screen" hint on iPhone
   Safari (the platform gives no install banner).

## How to verify

```bash
node --test tests/*.test.js            # 272 tests
python3 -m http.server 4173            # then open http://127.0.0.1:4173/
```

On first open you should see the welcome sheet; after it, the bottom bar on a
phone with Bottles, Shelf, Tastings, Night and More; "weller" in Bottles should
list each expression; a bottle card shows list price, MSRP, your average and
the club's rating with no verdict. Switch on More → Price check · beta and the
same Weller 12 at $65 reads Buy with "Allocated-bottle economics" first.
